How it works

One master product record

Brand, type, features, material, size, price, HSN, GST, manufacturer address, images, keywords, variants. Import the sheet you already use on Meesho or Flipkart.

Generate every channel

Amazon gets a 75-character Item Name and a 125-character Item Highlights line; Flipkart a ~100-character title; Meesho a 60–80-character keyword-led name with no MRP claims; ShopClues, Shopsy and IBI Marketplace their own.

Score, fix, polish

Every field gets a length meter and a compliance check; a 0–100 score tells you what is missing. Edit anything (it locks), or let AI rewrite it — the engine re-checks the result.

Export the exact sheet

Amazon flat-file columns, Flipkart's listing sheet, Meesho's catalogue sheet, ShopClues, IBI's bulk template, Shopify and WooCommerce CSVs — one row per variant.

Close the loop

Log impressions, clicks and orders per live title. Words that ride with higher click-through move earlier next time; weak words move later. Versions are kept.

Add any marketplace

The Custom Channel builder lets you define limits, rules, title slots and sheet columns for a platform we have not added yet — no code.

Questions

Does it upload to the marketplaces for me?

Not yet. It produces each marketplace's own bulk sheet, which you upload in Seller Central / Seller Hub / Supplier Panel in one step. Direct API publishing is on the roadmap for sellers who authorise it.

Does it work without an account?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser and stays there. An account adds cloud sync across devices, server-side AI and paid plans.

Where does the AI come from?

On paid plans the service uses a hosted model with a monthly quota. On any plan you can paste your own Gemini API key, which never leaves your device. In both cases the rule engine re-validates every field the model writes, so nothing goes out over a limit or with a banned word.

Are the platform rules kept current?

Each channel carries the date of the policy it encodes, and updates ship as marketplaces change their rules. Custom channels let you adjust a limit yourself the same day.

What about Legal Metrology?

Manufacturer / packer / importer name and address, common name, net quantity, MRP, country of origin and consumer care contact are part of the product record, checked per channel and exported into the columns that need them.
